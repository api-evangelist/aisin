The automotive industry is exploring various options, including electric vehicles, to achieve carbon neutrality. A new option in this trend is the development of hydrogen-powered vehicles. To accelerate this effort, Toyota Motor Corporation competed in a race in 2021 with a vehicle fueled by compressed gaseous hydrogen. In addition, in 2023, Toyota participated in a race with the world’s first liquid hydrogen-fueled machine and successfully completed the race.
